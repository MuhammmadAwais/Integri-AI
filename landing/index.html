FAQ

Questions about the free AI chat app

Is Integri really free?

Yes. Every service that can be offered for free is free in Integri, supported by ads. That includes chatting with 30+ AI models, multi chat, file analysis and file creation, voice chat, image generation, personalized news, custom AI agents and the product finder.

Is Integri a free ChatGPT alternative?

Yes. Integri is a free AI chat app that lets you use GPT models together with Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok and many more. Everyday chat is free with ads, and Premium cuts ads to almost zero for $10 a month.

Can I use Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek for free?

Yes. In Integri you can chat with 30+ AI models for free, including Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek, supported by ads, and switch between them in the same app.

Claude vs ChatGPT vs Gemini: which one is best?

It depends on the task. Many people prefer Claude for long texts and careful writing, GPT models for all round everyday help, and Gemini for long documents and research. The easiest way to decide is to ask all three the same question with multi chat in Integri and compare the answers side by side.

What is the best AI model for writing, coding or studying?

There is no single winner, and the best model changes as new versions launch. In Integri you can try the leading models for writing, coding and studying in one place, compare their answers and keep the one that works best for you.

Is there a cheaper alternative to ChatGPT Plus?

Yes. Integri Premium costs $10 a month, or $100 a year, and gives you 30+ leading AI models in one app with almost no ads. The free plan already includes most features.

Can I chat with a PDF for free?

Yes. Upload any PDF or document to get a summary, ask questions about it, or turn the answer into a new PDF or Word file. This AI PDF summarizer is part of the free plan.

Is the AI image generator free?

Yes. Every user can create and edit images for free. Premium and Pro subscribers get higher quality pictures.

Can AI turn text into video?

Yes. The AI text to video generator in Integri Premium and Pro turns a short description into a video clip. Video generation needs far more computing power than ads can cover, so it is part of the paid plans.

Can I build a custom AI agent for free?

Yes. You can create your own AI agent for any task and choose which of the 30+ models powers it. Subscribers can create more agents.

Can AI compare prices for me?

Yes. The AI shopping assistant in Integri compares products and prices across shops and shows the best offers in the chat. You can also buy mobile top ups and gift cards directly in the app.

Is there an AI app with no ads?

Integri Premium keeps ads to a bare minimum, close to zero, for $10 a month or $100 a year. It includes every free feature plus higher quality images, video generation and more AI agents.

How much does Integri Premium cost?

Premium costs $10 a month or $100 a year. Pro costs $20 a month or $200 a year and adds higher usage limits. Both cut ads to almost zero and let you create more AI agents.

Is there an AI chat app for iPhone and Android?

Yes. You can download the Integri AI chat app for iPhone from the App Store and for Android from Google Play, or use it in your browser at integri.app.

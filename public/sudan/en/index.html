Frequently asked questions

Do I need to download the app?

No, you can top up directly from this page. The app is optional if you want to save numbers and repeat top ups easily.

Which networks are available?

We top up Zain, MTN and Sudani numbers in Sudan.

How long does the credit take to arrive?

The credit arrives instantly after the payment is confirmed, and you can follow the order status in the app history.

Which amounts are available?

You can choose an amount between 2 and 6.20 euro. Next to each amount we show the units the number receives and the dollar price, both approximate, and the final price is shown clearly before you pay.

Which payment methods are available?

Bank or credit card with 3D Secure, Apple Pay, Google Pay and other methods shown for your country at payment.

Where can I top up from?

From any country outside Sudan. The credit arrives on the number in Sudan.

What if I enter a wrong number?

The credit is sent instantly to the number you entered and cannot be recovered after delivery, so check the number and the network before paying. If the top up fails and the credit does not arrive, contact us at support@integri.app.

How do I recharge Zain Sudan online from abroad?

Choose Zain, then the amount, enter the Zain number in Sudan and your email, and pay. The credit arrives instantly. The same steps work for MTN and Sudani.

Can I top up Zain or Sudani from Europe?

Yes, you can top up Zain, MTN or Sudani in Sudan from Europe or anywhere outside Sudan, and the credit arrives instantly.

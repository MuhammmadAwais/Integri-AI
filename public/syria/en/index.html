Frequently asked questions

Do I need to download the app?

No, you can top up directly from this page. The app is optional if you want to save numbers and repeat top ups easily.

How do I transfer balance to MTN Syria from abroad?

Choose the amount, enter the MTN number in Syria and your email, and pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The credit arrives instantly on the number.

How long does the credit take to arrive?

The credit arrives instantly after the payment is confirmed, and you can follow the order status in the app history.

Which payment methods are available?

Bank or credit card with 3D Secure, Apple Pay, Google Pay and other methods shown for your country at payment.

Where can I top up from?

From anywhere outside Syria. The credit arrives on the MTN number in Syria.

What if I enter a wrong number?

The credit is sent instantly to the number you entered and cannot be recovered after delivery, so check the number before paying. If the top up fails and the credit does not arrive, contact us at support@integri.app.

Which MTN Syria bundles are available?

We offer 500, 600, 700, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000, 3,500 and 4,500 units. Choose the bundle you need from the list at the top of the page.

How much does an MTN Syria top up cost?

MTN Syria top up prices start from 5.45 euro for the 500 unit bundle. The final price is shown clearly before you pay, with no hidden fees.

Can I send MTN credit to Syria from Europe?

Yes, you can send MTN credit to any number in Syria from Europe or anywhere outside Syria, and it arrives instantly.
